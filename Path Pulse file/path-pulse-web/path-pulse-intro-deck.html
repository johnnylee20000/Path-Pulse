PATH-PULSE

Turn every walk into a mission. GPS routes, metabolic diagnostics, fuel tracking, and Ghost-Path racing—in your browser, Obsidian Lab aesthetic.

SUMMARY

What is Path-Pulse?

The product

A progressive web app that treats movement like tactical data: map expeditions, log fuel & steps, track BMI and weekly missions—without an app store install.

The experience

Explorer’s Oath → PRISM welcome → Home, Map, Report, Profile. Neon cyan & lime on obsidian—built for clarity and shareable “lab” vibes.
