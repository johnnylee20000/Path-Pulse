       FOR ALL FULL TIME SPECIAL RESERVE POLICE OFFICERS 
 
Guidelines for Completing SRP Assessment Form 2025 
1. Accuracy and Completeness 
- Fill out all Sections of this form in BLOCK LETTERS using Black or Blue ink. 
- Ensure no fields are left blank. If a section/question is not applicable, please indicate 
clearly as ‘N/A’. 
- Ensure that all Information stated is true and correct to the best of your knowledge. 
 
2. Supporting Documentation 
           You must attach certified copies of all original supporting documents including: - 
- Police Identification Card 
- Personal Identification: - (Certificate of Birth, Identification Card, Driver’s License, 
Marriage Certificate) 
            -    Certificates, Diplomas and/or Training Credentials  
            -    Exemption letters    
            -    Commendations 
            -    Prior Service records (if applicable) 
 NOTE: Originals must be made available for inspection and certification by your SDO i/c. 
3. Proper Endorsement and Sign-Off 
- Section A to E must be filled out by the Applicant. 
- Section F must be completed by the Supervisor only. 
- Section G must be filled out by the Head of Division/Section/Branches only. 
- Divisional Officer must Review, Verify and Sign-off on the Assessment Form. 
- Ensure that all required Signatures, Stamps and Dates are properly affixed. 
Forms without proper endorsement will be considered incomplete and will be returned. 
4. Submission Protocol 
         Your completed forms must be forwarded through your Head of Division/Section/Branch                       
along with; 
- All the attached documents that should be stamped and verified by your SDO i/c 
Final submissions must reach the Office of the Adjutant no later than Monday 4th August, 
2025 by 4:00pm.
