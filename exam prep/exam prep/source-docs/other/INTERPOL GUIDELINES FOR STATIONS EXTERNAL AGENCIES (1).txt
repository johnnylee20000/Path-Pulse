GUIDELINES FOR STATIONS/EXTERNAL AGENCIES 
RE: REQUEST FOR INFORMATION E.G. FOREIGN C/R CHECKS. 
 
A Memo from the SDO i/c (or requesting agency) addressed to the SDO i/c INTERPOL, along 
with brief facts; OR a minute from the SDO i/c attached to the investi gator’s request.  The 
following should be included (where applicable) within:  
 
 Full/proper names (especially for Spanish nationals) / aliases AND Date of Birth; 
 Whether subject is charged (state offence/s) OR detained; 
 Where charged, copy of fingerprints; OR CR Number; OR Fingerprint Transaction 
Number (where KO # is yet to be generated); 
 Copy of Identity Document/s; 
 Photo of detainee on arrest;  
 Number/Rank/Name/Contact Info incl. cell phone and email (preferably a TTPS 
account), of the investigator; 
 Court of hearing and postponement date; 
 Reason/purpose for request, and other info deemed necessary to the investigation. 
 
These are necessary in order to: 
 Send a detailed request to the relevant jurisdiction/agency, with a view of receiving 
comprehensive feedback; 
 Input into INTERPOL databases. 
  
ALL emails must be sent via official TTPS account ONLY. 
With the exception of an exigency, NO requests emanating from personal email accounts 
will be entertained.
