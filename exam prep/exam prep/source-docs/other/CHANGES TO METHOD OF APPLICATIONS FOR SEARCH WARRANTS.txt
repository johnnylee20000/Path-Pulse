Information received from Ms. Marsha Chasseau, Assistant Registrar & Deputy Marshal, 
Judiciary of the Republic of Trinidad and Tobago Hall of Justice, Knox Street, Port of Spain:  
Please be informed that with effect from 8:00 am on Friday 12th, July, 2024, applications to 
the High Court (Criminal Division) for Search Warrants under the Administration of Justice 
(Indictable Proceedings) Act 2011 must be forwarded to the following designated email 
addresses: 
  
sw.north@ttlawcourts.org      (Applications from Trinidad North) 
sw.south@ttlawcourts.org      (Applications from Trinidad South) 
sw.tobago@ttlawcourts.org    (Applications from Tobago) 
  
For clarity, applications for search warrants are no longer to be forwarded to the email 
address presently in use. 
  
Further note that any application for which urgent consideration is sought must include the word 
“URGENT” in the email caption. Where an urgent application is made outside the regular 
business hours and/or days of the Court, the applicant must contact the Assistant Registrar by 
telephone once the application has been submitted. 
  
Further note the attached sample form for a Search Warrant. 
  
Applications for Search Warrants that are not accompanied with the relevant documentation, not 
in proper form, lacking in information or content, or transmitted incorrectly, may be delayed in 
their processing or not granted.
