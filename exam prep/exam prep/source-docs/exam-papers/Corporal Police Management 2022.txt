MINI CASE No.1
You are the Corporal in charge of a shift in the Cocorite Police Station in which there is a
surge in crime in the district. The Inspector in charge of the Station held a CompStat meeting
and has identified your shift as one requiring for priority attention. The Inspector tells you
that most of the serious crimes occur during the night when your shift is on duty and the
response time is poor. Furthermore, the detection rate for your shift is the lowest in the
station. He further states that your shift is lagging on submission of case files on serious
crime investigations. The Inspector mandates you to meet your staff as a priority and plan
effective responses to the current issues plaguing your shift and submit your proposals in
two weeks’ time.
Following the meeting with the Inspector, you meet with your staff on the next daylight
shift and describe your meeting with the Inspector. You relay the Inspector’s concern about
being made to look bad in the eyes of the Executive of the Division, and that he does not
like that. During the meeting your tone is aggressive and threatening and you refuse the
officers attempts at feedback. After the meeting, the members of the shift grumble that this
is not your usual method of communicating with them and that no one was clear about the
issues identified by the Inspector.
(A) Identify the leadership style which you adopted as the Corporal.
(B) Describe the leadership style that would assist the Corporal in his interactions with
his staff.
(C)  ILlustrate, using headed arrows, the communication pattern existing among the
three (3) levels of this Station.
(D) List two (2) barriers to communication between the Corporal and the staff which
were intensified by the tone adopted.
(E) Recommend two (2) approaches the Corporal could have used in the staff meeting
to ensure effective communication
