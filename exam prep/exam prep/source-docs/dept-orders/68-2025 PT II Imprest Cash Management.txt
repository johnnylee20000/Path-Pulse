PART I
IMPREST CASH MANAGEMENT MANUAL
1. INFORMATION
The purpose of this manual is to guide officers of the Trinidad and Tobago Police
Services (TTPS) in the use and accounting procedures of imprest cash issued to
Divisions/Branches/Sections/Units of the TTPS. (Appendix I)
2. LEGISLATIVE AUTHORITY WHICH GUIDES IMPREST CASH
a) Exchequer and Audit Act, Chapter 69:01, Part XIII, ‘Imprest’ para 106;
b) Exchequer and Audit Act, Chapter 69:01, Part VIII, Payments of Public Moneys
para 72-79;
c) Financial Instructions 1965 Part XI (Imprest: Para 175-194); and
d) Trinidad and Tobago Police Service Standing Order 43 Financial
Administration Part !I, ‘Imprest’, para 40-45.
3. DEFINITIONS
“Accounting Officer” is defined in the Exchequer and Audit Act, Chapter 69:01, Part
I, Section 2 as “any person appointed by the Treasury and charged with the duty of
accounting for any service in respect of which moneys have been appropriated by the
constitution or by Parliament, or any person to whom issues are made from the
‘Exchequer Account’.
“Authorized Personnel” means Internal Auditors of the Trinidad and Tobago Police
Service and personnel attached to the Trinidad and Tobago Police Service Finance
Branch and posted to the Estimates Section.
“Comptroller of Accounts” means to ensure that all “Imprests’ are duly accounted for
in accordance with the terms of their issue.
“Financial Administration” means the Commissioner of Police, Trinidad and Tobago
Service.
“Financial Manager” means the Officer in charge of the Finance Branch, Trinidad and
Tobago Police Service.

4. PURPOSE OF IMPREST CASH/IMPREST CASH MANAGEMENT
This manual is further revised to inform all officers in charge of the Divisions/Branches/
Units, who have been designated with the additional responsibility as “Imprest’
Holders, towards prudent alignment as it relates to the expending and accounting of
“Imprest’ Cash under their charge.
This is to avoid any incident of misuse, misappropriation, fraudulent conversion and
disregard of the guidelines as outlined in the Exchequer and Audit act, Chap. 69:01,
Financial Regulations 1965 and the Trinidad and Tobago Police Service Standing Order
No. 43, Financial Administration.
As such, careful consideration must be given as to the correct/precise conditions under
which “Imprest” Cash can be expended, coupled with the effective and efficient posting
of the “Imprest Cash Book”.
5. OBJECTIVE
The development of this manual ts to provide guidance to “Imprest” Holders, within
the Trinidad and Tobago Police Service, to effectively and efficiently carry out the
functions of one’s office and ensure that they acquire a better understanding and
appreciation of the need to properly account for “Imprest Cash” expended and the
maintenance of the “Imprest Cash Book” in their possession.

6. PURPOSE OF IMPREST

1. The purpose of the “Imprest” is for use in cases of emergency, where a purchase
order cannot be made readily available due to unforeseen circumstances. Such
miscellaneous expenses, require immediate attention in order to ensure the
continuous operation of the TTPS.

2. In the case of Vehicle Imprest, this is to be used in support of crime detection for
covert operations only. Where time is crucial, and it isn’t always prudent to have a
supplier available in order to obtain rented vehicle or where there may be a need to
change a vehicle during an operation in order to preserve the identity of a
confidential informant as well as officers whilst gathering intelligence on field
operations.

3. In the case of Informant Imprest, same is to be used for the gathering of intelligence
in appropriate levels of reward payments when applied to a Covert Human
Intelligence Sources (CHIS) associated with cultivating informants by their
handlers.

4. In the case of Crime Imprest same is to be used for various policing operations in
keeping with 6 (1) as stated above.

5. Payment from an imprest shall not be made except for the purpose for which the
imprest was issued.

6. Payment of wages and salaries to regularly employed persons shall not be made
from petty cash.

7. WHAT IS IMPREST
‘Imprest’ refers to cash advanced by the Commissioner of Police to an officer in charge
of a Division/Branch/Section/Unit in order to meet “petty expenditure’ that may arise
from time to time in the course of his/her official duties. This cash is distributed from
the Minister of Finance upon application and in consideration of its necessity for the
proper functioning of his/her office, via the Comptroller of Accounts, to enable that
person to carry out the business of the department.

TYPE OF IMPREST

“Irredeemable Imprest” — refers to ‘Imprests’ that are not required to be retired at the

end of each financial year (30th September), but al! payments for such ‘Imprests’ shall!

be brought to account in the year in which the transaction occurred.

IMPREST HOLDER

(i). In the Trinidad and Tobago Police Service, the Commissioner of Police has sole

responsibility to advance a specified amount of cash to an officer in charge of
Divisions/Branches/Sections/Units. This officer who is deemed as the “Imprest
Holder” based on their needs, can meet petty expenditures that may arise from
time to time in the course his/her official duties. (In accordance with the
instructions as outlined in the Trinidad and Tobago Police Service Standing
Orders 43 Part [I “Financial Administration”).

ii). An officer in charge of a Division/Branch/Section/Unit where an “Imprest’ has
been designated by the Commissioner of Police, must take charge of that

‘Imprest’ and ensure that the ‘Imprest Cash Book” is properly well updated.

8. RESPONSIBILITY OF THE IMPREST HOLDER

(i). Exchequer and Audit Act, Chap. 69:01 outlines the “Imprest Holder’s”
responsibility as “An Imprest Holder” is personally responsible for the
disbursement of cash of his ‘Imprest’.

(ii). | He/She shall check the ‘Imprest’ regularly and frequently; and maintain it in
such a way that it is readily verifiable by authorized personnel.

(iii). Ensure items are purchased and a receipt is obtained/submitted for all cash
transactions.

(iv). Keep a record of all such transactions and secure the receipt for verification.

(v). Immediately submit sub-vouchers for transaction during any month along with
a statement to the officer in charge of Finance Branch for redemption.

(vi). Balance the “Imprest Cash Book” on the following occasions:

(a) when all sub-vouchers are being redeemed;

(b) when the “Imprest” is being retired/returned from the Finance Branch;

(c) when the cash balance of an “Irredeemable Imprest” is being carried
forward at the end of the year and the “Honour Certificate” submitted to
the officer in charge of Finance Branch.

(vii). When handing over charge of an ‘Imprest’ to another Officer:

(a) check the ‘Imprest’ cash and sub-vouchers on hand, prepare a handover
in the ‘Imprest cash book” and ensure the handover is certified and
signed by both officers (the handing over and taking over officers);

(b) be personally accountable for the amount of his/her “Imprest’.

(c) check ‘Imprest’ regularly and frequently, and maintain it in such a way
that it is readily verifiable by “Authorized Officers” so to do.

(viii). In addition to the above responsibilities states, “Imprest Holder’s” must cash
redemption cheques issued in his/her name as soon as it is received.

9. IMPREST CASH BOOK

(i). Where an ‘Imprest’ is issued for the purpose of meeting petty cash expenses, an

“Imprest Cash Book” in the form approved by the Treasury shall be kept and

maintained in the manner directed by the Treasury. The significance of the

“Imprest Cash Book’ is to indicate or show a record of all transactions

undertaken during the course of receipts, payment and redemption of petty cash.

No receipts of cash, other than that received when the ‘Imprest’ is issued and

when sub-vouchers are redeemed, shall be entered in the cash book or included

with the ‘Imprest’ cash.

10. SUB VOUCHERS AND RECEIPTS
The use of sub-vouchers and receipts are essential in redeeming ‘Imprest’.
(i). NUMBERING OF SUB-VOUCHERS
Sub-vouchers relating to each sub-item of expenditure shal! be numbered
consecutively and the respective entries in the cash book numbered accordingly.
The number of each sub-item shall start at one (1) at the beginning of each
financial year (1*' October).
(ii). REDEMPTION OF SUB-VOUCHERS
Sub-vouchers shall be redeemed as often as necessary but not less than twice
per month. As soon as payments are effected, efforts must be made to have sub-
vouchers redeemed before the end of the said month.

11. CERTIFICATE OF PAYMENT

In extraordinary instances, where it may be impracticable to obtain receipts of petty

disbursements of a machine generated bill for the purchase of an item and prior

approval for expending ‘Imprest Cash’ is received from the officer in charge of the

Finance Branch, the ‘Imprest Holder” is required to complete a certificate of payment

and affixed to the sub-vouchers at Appendix II.

12. CHECKING OF IMPREST CASH BOOK

The ‘Imprest Cash Book” shall be checked at least once weekly and at the close of

each month by the “Imprest Holder” checking officer who shall furnish the

undermentioned statement and certificate at Appendix III.

13. HANDING OVER IMPREST

(1) The officer in charge of a Division/Branch/Section/Unit prior to leaving office
whether via transfer/retirement, shall make arrangements and hand over
responsibility as “Imprest Holder” to the officer taking charge of that said office.
If that officer is unavailable, he/she shall hand over charge of the ‘Imprest’ to a
responsible officer of his/her department or of another department who shall
check the ‘Imprest Cash’, ‘Imprest Cash Book’ and sub-vouchers on hand with
the officer handing over and sign receiving same. A Handover must be done in
accordance with Standing Order No. 9 para 5,6 and 8.

STANDING ORDER NO. 9
(1) It shall be the duty of the First Division Officer handing over and the

First Division Officer taking over a Division/Branch/Section/Unit to
prepare the “handover” in a manner as set out in Appendix IV of this
Order.

(2) They shall also check the” Imprest Cash Book”, cash on hand and
vouchers and sign a certificate as set out in Appendix III.

(ii). The Handover Certificate shall be prepared in quadruplicate, each officer
retaining a copy and the original dispatched to the Commissioner of Police; the
fourth copy in the case of the Executive Officers, shall be placed in the
Division/Branch/ Section/Unit Handover File.

(iii). Where for any reason an officer is unable to handover his
Division/Branch/Section/Station due to death, serious illness or unforeseen
circumstances, it shall be:

(i) In the case of a First Division Officer, the responsibility of the Assistant
Commissioner in charge of such First Division Officer to supervise the
taking over of such Division/Branch/Section.

14. EXAMINATION OF IMPREST CASH

Authorized personnel of the Finance Branch are required to make periodic checks at

least once every quarter and are not obligated to announce his/her intended visit. The

officer shall identify himself/herself on arrival to the “Imprest Holder” or “Imprest Cash

Clerk” and must be granted access to audit the “Imprest” Cash and “Imprest Cash

Book” and is required to sign the “Imprest Cash Book” at the concluding of the

audit/examination.

The purpose of the spot check is to:
(a) ensure compliance with Financial Regulations and Instructions;
(b) ensure “Imprest” cash is properly accounted for and the “Imprest Cash
Book” is correctly posted.
(c) ensure there is no discrepancy in the recording, holding or securing of
cash. Any discrepancy between the balance shown in the cash record
and the actual in hand shall be immediately reported for investigation.
DISCREPANCY
Any discrepancy discovered in the recording, keeping and accounting of cash is the
responsibility of the “Imprest Holder”. Shortages must be made good by the “Imprest
Holder”.
15. INTERNAL AUDIT
Authorized Personnel from the Internal Audit Department, Trinidad and Tobago Police
Service are required to make periodic checks of “Imprest” Cash held at respective
Divisions/Branches/Sections/Units as often as necessary within each financial year, in
order to ensure proper accountability of utilized of “Imprest” cash, in compliance with
Financial Regulations and Instructions.
16. HONOUR CERTIFICATE (RE: CASH HELD)
Holders of irredeemable “Imprest” are required to submit to the officer in charge of the
‘Finance Branch’ Trinidad and Tobago Police Service at the end of each financial year
(30th September), the “Honour Certificate” attesting to the cash retained in the
Division/Branch/Section/Unit. All sub-vouchers on hand must be forwarded together
with the “Honour Certificate”. This information is compiled to be forwarded to the
Comptroller of Accounts as indicated at Appendix V.
17. PAYMENTS FROM IMPREST
(1) It must be further emphasized that payments from an “Imprest” shall not be
made except for the purpose of which the “Imprest” was issued;

(2) ‘{mprest’ Cash must not be used for the following:

(a) Payment of wages and salaries;

(b) Minor Equipment purchases;

(c) Office stationary;

(d) Hosting of small functions or events;

(e) Conducting Identification Parade;

(f) Repairs to office equipment and vehicle;

(g) Un-official expenses;

(h) Groceries and food/bakery items for staff functions/conferences.

(3) (1) Whenever any extraordinary events occur, to alleviate all doubt, contact must
be made with the officer in charge of the Trinidad and Tobago Police Service,

Finance Branch, to get approval before the expending of “Imprest” Cash. An

appended report must be included when submitting the sub-voucher and include

a) Description of article purchased;

(ii) In the instance where a receipt could not be obtained, a report countersigned
by at least two persons verifying that a transaction had taken place and it was
not possible to obtain a receipt.

18. MAINTENANCE OF THE IMPREST CASH

1. The “Imprest” Cash Book has two (2) sides, the left side and the right side.

(i) The left side/debit side contains six (6) columns:

(a) in column marked DATE - write in chronological order, the

date of each posting for the said month.

(ii) In the column marked PARTICULARS:

(a) the first entry under this heading write: “Balance Brought
Forward” (Note: the balance brought forward should include
cash on hand and uncashed cheques) (if applicable);

(b) List in date order, the cheques along with the cheque numbers
and the sub-voucher numbers to which the cheque represents,
under the sub heading “Receipts” (Note: This is the cheque
number(s) for the replenishment cheque(s) and the number(s) for
the associated sub-voucher(s) that are being reimbursed;

{c) Add the amount received on the cheques to the amount brought
forward. In the following lines state:

(i) Total receipts;

(ii) Total payments (monies spent);

(iii) Cash on hand;

(iv) Vouchers in transit: VR# - Date — Amount.

(iii) In the column marked AMOUNT RECEIVED:

(a) The first entry under the heading write the amount of cash that
was brought forward into the new month (i.e. which was held at
the close of the previous month);

(b) Write the amount received after having cashed the cheque listed
in the ‘particulars column’;

(c) Total the amount of cash spent for the month. Write this figure
in the line new to (Total Payments), the difference/balance
Should be listed as cash on hand which forms part of the
certificate on the right side.

(iv) In the column marked DATE - write the date of the transaction for
which the ‘Imprest ‘was used.

(v) In the column marked SUB-VOULCHER NUMBER - write the number
consecutively to each sub-item of expenditure and the respective entries
in the cash book numbered accordingly. The number of each sub-item
head shall start at one (1) at the beginning of that financial year.

(vi) In the column marked PARTICULARS — show the nature of the
transaction for which the “Imprest’ was used, e.g. (purchase of
newspapers).

2. The right side/credit side contains four (4) columns:

(i) In the column marked AMOUNT PAID — write the cost of the item for
which the “Imprest’ was used;

(ii) Under the column marked DISTRIBUTION -— list the sub-item which
coincides with the transaction, e.g. (purchase of newspaper — sub-item
(II) Books and Periodicals). Summation of each sub-items under the
distribution column must give the value of the summation under amount
paid column.

(iit) In the column marked DATE SENT TO TREASURY ~— write the date
that the vouchers were sent to the Finance Branch for
reimbursement/redemption.

(iv) In the column marked DATE OF REDEMPTION - write the date that
the reimbursement cheque was received. On the right side of the
“Imprest’ Cast book and on, the same line with total receipt, a summary
of the “Imprest cash for that particular month should be furnished at
Appendix VI.
