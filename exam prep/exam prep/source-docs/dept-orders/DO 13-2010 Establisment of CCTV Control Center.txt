CCTV Control Centre 
A Closed Circuit Television Control Centre (CCTV) has been established in the Trinidad and 
Tobago Police Service with effect from 1
51 
February 2009. 
The CCTV Control Centre is staffed by both Police and Civilian personnel a<; indicated: -
One ( 1) Police Inspector 
One (1) Police Corporal 
Three (3) Police Constables 
Four (4) Support Officers 
One (1) Civilian Supervisor 
Nine (9) Civilian Operators 
The Control Centre operates on a twenty-four hour basis. Access to which is Restricted. Only 
investigators officially assigned to matters requiring assistance from CCTV coverage will be 
allowed into the Centre. Investigators can make requests directly to the center, through the 
following contact numbers: -
Control Centre - 625-8097/623-0630 
Fax - 6231143 
Coordinator- 623-8489 
Supervisor- 624-4452 
At present there are cameras positioned in the following districts- Port of Spain, Woodbrook, St 
James, Belmont~ Besson Street, and San Fernando. Cameras are also strategically positioned 
along the Priority Bus Route from Port of Spain to Macoya and along the Churchill Roosevelt 
Highway/Beetham Highway/Wrightson Road/Audrey Jeffers Highway, enabling a viewing 
capacity from Piarco Airport in the east to Cocorite Flyover in the west. Maps identifying the 
CCTV camera locations within the respective policing districts will be distributed I collected at 
Crime and Problem Analysis Branch (CAP A) situated at Old Police Headquarters, Port of Spain. 
It is envisioned that the information provided will enlighten the ambitions of oflicers and would 
encourage them to utilize the system as far as practicable, to assist them whilst investigating 
reports.
