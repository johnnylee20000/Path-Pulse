ORGANIZATIONAL CHART AMENDMENT
2. The Organisational Chart of the Trinidad and Tobago Police Service at APPENDIX A
(Departmental Order No. 135 of 2019, dated Wednesday 31° July 2019) has been amended
to reflect changes in the following areas:
i. The Firearms Section, which currently functions under ACP Administration shall
continue to have direct operational responsibility to the Commissioner of Police.
e The Supplemental Police Department shall fall under the responsibility
and supervision of the ACP Administration and Support.
ii. The Special Victims Department falls under ACP Criminal Division.
e The Special Victims Department amalgamates the Child Protection Unit,
Gender Based Violence Unit and the newly formed Sexual Offences Unit
iii. The Cyber and Social Media Unit has been removed from the ACP White Collar
Division and now falls under the ACP Central Intelligence Bureau (C.L.B.).
» @ The Cyber and Social Media Unit has been merged with the Research,
Analytical Unit.
iv. Rapid Response Unit comprises ERP and E999/Command Center.
® The Emergency Response Patrol (ERP) has been incorporated to be part of
the Emergency 999/Command Centre (E999/CC).
e The ERP, E999 Command Center remains under ACP Specialized Support
