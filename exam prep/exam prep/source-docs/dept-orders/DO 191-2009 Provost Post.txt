PART II 
1. On 22nd September 2009, the Post ofProvost of the Trinidad and 
Tobago Police Service was established . The Board of the Government 
Human Resource Services Ltd has approved the engagement of Mr. 
Robert Stephen Watt (CMLS Consulting Services Ltd.) to this post for a 
period of three years. 
The Provost of the Trinidad and Tobago Police Training Academy shall be 
responsible for the overall administration and operation of the Academy, 
and will develop and customise a modern, leading edge police training 
programme for delivery to recruits and in-service officers in Trinidad and 
Tobago. The intent is to ensure that the Police Academy becomes the 
regional leader in police training~ and has best practices and procedures 
that are commensurate with training academies in developed countries. 
The Provost will report directly to the Commissioner of Police.
