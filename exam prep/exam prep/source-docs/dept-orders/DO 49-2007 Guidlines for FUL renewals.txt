Departmental Order PART I- Nll... 
PART II 
1. In keeping with the requirements of the Firearms (Amendment) Act, 2004 
and The Firearms (Amendment) Regulations , 2004 all Firearm User's Licenses 
shall be valid for three years from the date of issue and may be renewed by the 
Commissioner ofPol ice upon the payment of a fee of$250.00. 
2. This means that all Firearms User's Licenses issued prior to 30th January 
2004 must be renewed. 
3. An applicant for the renewal of a Firearm User's License must submit the 
following documents along with his/her renewal application. 
(i) A certificate of competence issued by a Licensed Firearms 
Instructor approval by the Commissioner of Police ; 
(ii) A certificate of character; and 
(iii) A medical certificate of fitness issued by a Registered Medical 
Practitioner at the discretion of the Second Division Officer in charge of 
the station. 
4. It should be noted that serving members of the Protective Service , Defence 
Force, Customs and Cadets are exempted from the conditions listed at (i), (ii) and 
(iii) above. 
5. Heads of Divisions must ensure that this Order is strictly adhered to. 
6. Attached as at Appendix "A' to this Order is a list of the Accredited 
Firearms Institutions/Instructors.
