Departmental Order 
PART 1I 
AMENDMENT TO STANDING ORDERS N0s.17 and 38 
INFORMATION 
The Executive of the Trinidad and Tobago Police Service in an effort to ensure the delivery of 
professional service to the public has initiated the issuance of a prescribed receipt to persons who 
make reports at Police Stations. This initiative has become necessary as a result of numerous 
complaints by members of the public that no official records of their reports are documented in 
the Station Diary. The veracity of these complaints reflects negatively on the professional image 
of the Police Service. 
INTENTION 
This order is intended to amend Standing Orders Nos. 17 and 38, by establishing the issuance of 
'prescribed receipts ' to persons making reports and to apprise police officers as to their duties 
and responsibilities in keeping with such Standing Orders. 
AMENDMENTS : 
Standing Order No. 17 is amended as foUows· 
By deleting paragraph 4(e) and inserting the foUowing new paragraph: 
4(e) Make an entry of the report directly in the Station Diary or on the Prescribed 
Incident Report Forms, issue a 'prescribed receipt' to the person making the 
report and give information as to the possible cause of action . 
By deleting paragraph 6(b) and inserting the foUowing new paragraph: 
6 (b) the name and address of the person making the report , the time, date 
and place of the incident , including a brief detailed report and the serial 
number of the 'prescribed receipt ' issued to the person making the report .

Standing Order No. 38 is amended as follows: 
By deleting paragraph 7(c) and inserting the foUowing new paragraph: 
7(c) ensure that-
ADMINISTRATION 
i. all reports received during his tour of duty are recorded in 
the Station Diary or on the Prescribed Incident Report Forms 
and in the relevant corresponding books and/or registers; and 
ii. the 'prescribed receipt' is issued to the person making the 
report. 
The prescribed receipt books would be stocked at the Quarter Master Stores. The Second 
Division Officer in charge of stations shall requisition the said receipt books and ensure that 
adequate supplies are kept at station. 
:METHODOLOGY 
I. A police officer who receives a report shall enter it in the Station Diary or on the 
Prescribed Incident Report Forms and fill out the 'prescribed receipt' as set out in 
Appendix "A". 
2. The Second Division Officer in charge of the Charge Room/ Reception Area shall: 
a. Ensure that the 'prescribed receipt' is filled out by the officer receiving the report 
and signed by the person making the report; and 
b. Certify the prescribed receipt and issue the original copy to the person making the 
report. 
COMMUNICATION 
Heads of Divisions and Branches shall ensure that the information contained herein is brought to 
the attention of all officers under their command. 
AUTHORITY 
Regulation 193 (I) (b) of the Police Service Regulations 2007.
