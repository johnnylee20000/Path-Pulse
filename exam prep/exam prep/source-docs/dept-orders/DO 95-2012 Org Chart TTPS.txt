1. Organization Chart for the Trin idad and Tobago Police Service 
The reporting structure of the Trinidad and Tobago Police Service has been reviewed and 
a new Organization Chart has been developed to reflect the current lines of reporting 
responsibility. Attached is a copy of the Organization Chart . Title changes have also 
been made to some positions. 
2. Deputy Commiss ioners of Police

follows :

4. Civilian Heads, Provost and Director, Public Affairs 
Included on the new Organization Chart are the civilian Heads , the Director of Public 
Affairs and the Provost. The persons in these positions all provide administrative and 
strategic support to, and on behalf of, the Commissioner of Police . 
Persons in the following positions report directly to the Commissioner of Police: 
• Head, Executive Management Services 
• Head, Finance and Accounts 
• Head, Human Resource 
• Head, Legal 
• Head, Internal Audit 
• Provost, Police Training Academy 
• Director , Public Affairs 
Persons in the following positions report directly to the Deputy Commissioner of Police , 
Organizational Development : 
• Head, Planning , Research and Project Implementation 
• Head, Administration 
• Head, Information Technology
