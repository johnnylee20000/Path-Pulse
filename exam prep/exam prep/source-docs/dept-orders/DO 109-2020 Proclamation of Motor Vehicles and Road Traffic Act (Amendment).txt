PART I
Re: The Proclamation of Motor Vehicles and Road Traffic (Amendment) Act, No. 15 of 2020
Reference is made to the caption.
In keeping with its mandate for the operationalization of the new Fixed penalty Traffic Ticketing System
and Demerit Points System under the Motor Vehicles and Road Traffic (Amendment) Act, 2017
(MVRTA, 2017), the Ministry of Works and Transport is pleased to advise that Cabinet has agreed to the
partial Proclamation of sections 7, 10, 11, 12, 13, 14, 15, 16, 18, 19, 20, 21, 22, 23, 25 and 26 of the
Motor Vehicles and Road Traffic (Amendment) Act No. 15 of 2020 (MVRTA, 2020) to come into
effect on Tuesday 23" June, 2020.
The MVRTA, 2020 was passed in Parliament and assented to on June 15, 2020, and provides, inter alia,
for the introduction of:
1. the Spot Speed Camera Enforcement System;
2. the Electronic Vehicle Registration and Radio Frequency Identification (RFID) Tagging;
3 Regulations under section 100 of the Motor Vehicles and Road Traffic Act, Chap. 48:50
(‘“MVRT Act, Chap. 48:50’) to include:
4. restrictions on the shaded or degrees of tint of the windscreens and windows of motor
vehicles; and
5 the introduction of an Accessible Parking Permit System for persons with disabilities or
other persons in special circumstances.

The MVRTA, 2020 also included miscellaneous amendments to the MVRTA 2017, particularly in relation
to the process for filing of a notice to contest, the manner in which matters are scheduled before the courts
and the introduction of Rules for the management of matters before the Traffic Courts. Accordingly,
sections 10, 11, 12, 13, 14, 15, 16, 18, 19, 20, 21, 22, 23 and 26 of the MVRTA, 2020 shall bring into
effect the miscellaneous amendments to the MVRTA, 2017 for the seamless operation of U-Turn Software
Management System for the listing and determination of court matters emanating from the New Fixed
Penalty Traffic Ticketing System and Demerit Points System.
It is to be noted that sections 7 and 25 of the MVRTA, 2020 provide for the modernization of the laws
regarding the use and enforcement of window film on motor vehicles. Section 7 of the MVRTA, 2020
provides for the repeal of section 23(1)(d) of the MVRTA, Chap. 48:50 and by the same token, section
25 of the MVRTA, 2020 provides for the introduction of Regulations under section 100 of the MVRTA,
Chap. 48:50 for restrictions on the permissible percentage of total visible light transmittance through the
glass and any material that may be applied or affixed to the windscreen or any other windows of a motor
vehicle. The Regulations are currently being finalized by the Ministry.
As such, please be advised that law enforcement officers are to cease the enforcement and issuance of
Fixed Penalty Notices for the traffic violation under section 23(1)(d) of the MVRTA, Chap. 48:50 with
effect from Tuesday 23" June, 2020.
Also, there will be a delay in the implementation of the Accessible Parking Permit System for persons
with disabilities as the Regulations are still under development.
Thank you for your continued co-operation and support on the successful launch of the U-Turn project.

Commissioner of Police
