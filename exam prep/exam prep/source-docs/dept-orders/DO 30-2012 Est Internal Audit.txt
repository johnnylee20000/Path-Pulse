Departmental Order PART I-NIL 
PART II 
INTERNAL AUDIT VISITS AT STATIONS 
1. Members of the Internal Audit will be visiting the various Police Stations periodically. Divisional 
Commanders are to facilitate these visits and advise personnel to be courteous and professional in 
their interaction with the Auditors. 
Internal Audit is a new unit established within the Trinidad and Tobago Police Service replacing 
the ones from Ministry of National Security because the Commissioner of Police is now the 
Accounting Officer. 
Additionally, personnel must be advised to obtain approval from the appropriate authority to 
entertain inspection of registers and visits to sensitive areas.
