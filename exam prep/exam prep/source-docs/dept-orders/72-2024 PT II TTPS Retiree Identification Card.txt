TTPS Retiree Identification Card
Information:
The Commissioner of Police has taken a strategic decision, following extensive consultation to
implement the issue of Police Retiree Identification Cards. It is commonly known that retired police
officers carry a wealth of experience, skills and knowledge gained during their years of service. The
retired Police Officer ID Card serves as a tangible token of appreciation for their dedication to
maintaining law and order. It symbolizes the community’s recognition of their sacrifices and
acknowledges the invaluable contribution they have made to public safety.
Criteria for Issue:
The ID card will be issued under the following circumstances:
e The retired officer must have

a) Separated from service in good standing (i.e.-; must not have been as a result of a
discharge/dismissal due to Disciplinary/Criminal matters)

b) Before such separation, was authorized by law to engage in or supervise the
prevention, detection, investigation, or prosecution of, any person for, any breaches
of the law, and had statutory powers of arrest;

c) Before such separation, served as a police officer for an aggregate of ten (10) years
or more;

d) Has not been officially found by a qualified medical professional to be unqualified
for reasons relating to mental health.

e Those officers who opted for optional retirement MUST have completed a minimum of ten
(10) years of honourable service.

Uses:
e Retired police officers can stay connected with colleagues and the law enforcement
community;
e Exclusive networking opportunities;
e Ability to stay up to date with law enforcement development and share experience which
can foster a sense of camaraderie, and enable the transfer of invaluable knowledge;
e Engaging in community outreach programs, school presentations and local events which can
reinforce positive relationships between law enforcement and the community
e In emergency situations, having a recognizable retiree identification card to be more readily
accepted as trusted individual during a crisis. Their experience and expertise can be
mobilized effectively in situations where additional support and guidance are needed.
Accessibility:
The designated Retiree must apply to the Commissioner of Police via the Senior Superintendent Human
Resource Branch for the Identification Card.
RETIRED POLICE OFFICERS SHOULD NOT ENGAGE IN ANY SITUATION THAT
MAY BE DANGEROUS AND SHOULD CONTACT EMERGENCY SERVICES IF SUCH
SITUATIONS OCCUR.
