PART II
Extension of Employment — Officers (SRP) exceeding the age of 60
The Trinidad and Tobago Police Service (TTPS) retains retired personnel to function as
Special Reserve Police (SRP) officers at various ranks on part-time, full-time and contractual
capacities.
The retirement age for Second Division Officers (SDOs) and First Division Officers (FDOs)
within the TTPS is 55 and 60 years respectively, however there exists several SRP officers
who have exceeded the age of sixty (60) years and continue to serve.
It is to be noted that numerous officers over the age of 60 are granted extension of service
from time to time on a case by case basis owing to the exigency of the service. It is therefore
recommended that all requests for continuation of employment, be done on an absolute needs
basis and must be accompanied with the following documents: -
1. Staff Report
2. Certificate of Fitness
3. Recommendation of Head of Division/Branch/Section
This has become necessary to justify the rehiring of officers over the age of 60 given the
strict budgetary and financial constraints at this point in time.
