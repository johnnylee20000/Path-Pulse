PART II 
POLICE SERVICE COMMISSION VISITS AT ST ATlONS 
Members of the Police Service Commission will be visiting the various Police Stations periodically . 
Divisional Commanders are to facilitate these visits and advise personnel to be courteous and 
professional in their interaction with the Commissioners . 
Additionally , personnel must be advised to obtain approval from the appropriate authority to entertain 
inspection of registers and visits to sensitive areas .
