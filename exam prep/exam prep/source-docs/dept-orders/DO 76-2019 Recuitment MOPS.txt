PART Il
RECRUITMENT OF APPLICANTS INTO THE MULTI OPTION POLICE SECTION
The Multi Option Police Section (MOPS) are in the process of recruiting suitable
members of the Trinidad and Tobago Police Service (TTPS). Persons who are interested
in becoming members are invited to submit an application.
Applicants will have to undergo physical and medical examinations. They must be
able to at least complete a one and a half (1%) mile run in 15 minutes maximum,
two (2) mile run according to APFT standards for their age group, three (3) pull ups
minimum, forty (40) sit ups minimum in 2 minutes, forty (40) push-ups minimum in
2 minutes and ten (10) parallel bar dips minimum in two (2) minutes in the first
assessment. Applicants should also be comfortable with heights exceeding sixty (60)
feet for abseiling and fast rope training and must not be claustrophobic.
Interested persons are asked to apply to the Assistant C ommissioner of Police, “Special
Branch” through their respective Heads of their Division/Branch to reach no later than
01.07.2019. For further information, applicants can contact the Multi Option Police
Section (MOPS) office at 621-3492 or 621-2203.
