No. 15 Departmental Order PART II
Consular Notification under the Vienna Convention on Consular Relations
This serves as a routine reminder to ensure that the Embassy is informed of all arrests of U.S citizens
under Article 36 of the Vienna Convention on Consular Relations Treaty which was signed by Trinidad
and Tobago on April 24"" 1963.
Per Article 36, “if she or he requests, the competent authorities of the receiving State shall, without delay
inform the consular post of the sending State if, within its consular district, a national of that State is
arrested or committed to prison or to custody pending trial or is detained in any other manner”.
