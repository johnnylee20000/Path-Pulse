PART II
Amendment to the Administration of Justice (Indictable Proceedings) Act 2011 (AJIPA)
Executive Summary
The Commissioner of Police seeks to keep the members of the Trinidad and Tobago Police Service
(TTPS) abreast regarding the recent proclamation of the AJIPAA.
The Administration of Justice (Indictable Proceedings) (Amendment) Act, 2024, referred to as
AJIPAA, was assented to on the 4th June 2024. This act further amends the Administration of Justice
(Indictable Proceedings) Act 2011 (Act No 20 of 2011) which provides for the abolition of
Preliminary Enquiries and for the conduct of initial and sufficiency hearings by a Master of the High
Court.
To this end, the TTPS wishes to advise of the key amendments:
1. Section 21(2) (a) of Act No 20 of 2011 was amended to include the word “given” to provide
for the wider admissibility of witness statements; and
2. Section 21 of the Act 20 of 2011 was amended to insert a new subsection ( 9), to include the
definition of “police officer” as contained in the Proceeds of Crime Act, Chapter 11:27. This
amendment is deemed essential as it expands the definition of “police officer” to include
officers from the Customs & Excise Division, the Board of Inland Revenue or any officer
of agency of the State, lawfully vested with investigative powers to these exercisable by a
police officer appointed under the Police Service Act.
