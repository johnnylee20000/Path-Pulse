No. 126 Departmental Order PART I — NIL PART II
ESTABLISHMENT OF PROCEDURES FOR GUARD AND EMERGENCY BRANCH AND
INTER-AGENCY TASK FORCE
Except otherwise instructed by the Commissioner of Police, Police Officers who hold the substantive
ranks or acting positions within the Second Division and who may be promoted or demoted within the
Second Division from time to time are to serve, commencing on 1‘ September, 2020, no more than five
(5) years collectively, during their service in the Second Division when attached to:
e Guard and Emergency Branch (GEB), and or
e Inter-Agency Task Force (IATF)
Further, where an officer serves any period of time at GEB or IATF, those periods of service when
combined must not amount to, no more than five (5) years while serving in the Second Division.
The First Division Officer (FDO) in charge of the aforementioned operational sections must ensure
compliance with this Departmental Order. Further the FDO i/c must submit quarterly returns of Second
Division Police Officers under his/her command to the Senior Superintendent Human Resource Branch.
