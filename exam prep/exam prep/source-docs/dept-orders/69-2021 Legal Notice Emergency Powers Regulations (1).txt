No. 69 Departmental Order PARTS I & HI-NIL
PART II,
Legal Notice 142 of 2021 — Emergency Powers Regulations, 2021.
I refer to the captioned subject matter and inform that the members of the Law Association of Trinidad
and Tobago in accordance with Regulation 4 (5) (b) whiclf exempts person(s) providing legal services to
require curfew pass, in light of this they must produce one of the undermentioned if stopped by a Police
Officer in uniform;
1. A valid Law Association of Trinidad and Tobago fdentification Card for the 2020-2021 Law term;
2. A valid Practicing Certificate for the 2020-2021 law Term with a form of National Identification;
3. A Practicing Certificate for the 2019-2020 Law Term together with a receipt evidencing payment
for the 2020-2021 Law Term as well as a form of National Identification; or
4. A Law Officer’s Certificate for the 2020-2021 Law Term.
You are to ensure that all persons under your command are sensitized to this matter.
Please be guided accordingly.
