No. 13 Departmental Order PART I- NIL
PART II
Paragraph 3:9 of Departmental Order No. 211 dated Tuesday 20 November 2007
With immediate effect an officer who has attained a Bachelor of Laws Degree (LLB) from an institution
recognized by the Accreditation Council of Trinidad and Tobago after Monday 20" January, 2020 shall
only be exempted from writing the law component of the Police Promotion Examination in the Second
Division where law is one of the subject of the examination to be written. As such, in case where the
Promotion Examination consist of Law and Police Duties, among other components, the officer shall be
awarded 35 points for the Law components only which shall then be dealt with in accordance with sub-
paragraph 3:8:4 of Departmental Order No. 211 dated Tuesday 20'" November, 2007 is hereby rescinded
with immediate effect.
In keeping with the aforementioned, the officer must apply in writing to be exempted from writing the
law components of the Police Promotion Examination.
