Departmental Order PART 1- NIL 
PART II 
Surrendering of Firearms when entering the 
Ministry of the Attorney General 
This Departmental Order seeks to inform Police Officers, that taking firearms into the Ministry 
of the Attorney General Building is strictly prohibited . Officers conducting business at this office 
may lodge their firearms at either the Central Police Station or Police Administration Building 
(Guard Unit). 
Officers will be required to surrender their firearms when entering the Ministry of the Attorney 
General Building .
