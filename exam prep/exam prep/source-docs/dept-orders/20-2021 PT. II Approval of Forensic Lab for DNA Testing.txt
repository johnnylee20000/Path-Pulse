SUBJECT: Amendment to the First Schedule of the Administration of Justice
(Deoxyribonucleic Acid) Act, Chapter 5:34 and approval of Forensic Laboratory
known as Caribbean Forensic Services by Notification.
Reference is made to the subject at caption.
Cabinet Minute No. 704 (2 Session) dated December 10, 2020, agreed to the amendment of the
First Schedule of the Administration of Justice (Deoxyribonucleic Acid) Act, Chap. 5:34 (DNA
Act). By Order, as follows:
a) the inclusion of two (2) accrediting bodies:
(i) ANSI national Accreditation Board, LLC (ANAB; and
(ii) American Association for laboratory Accreditation A2LA) and
b) that approval be granted by the Minister, by Notification, for the utilization of the forensic
laboratory known as Caribbean Forensic Services, for the purpose of obtaining forensic
Deoxyribonucleic Acid (DNA) services.
The Attorney General by Legal Notices No. 400 dated December 16, 2020 and No. 4 dated
January 11, 2021, gave effect to Cabinet’s decision at (a) and (b), copies of which are enclosed.
Submitted for your information and records.
