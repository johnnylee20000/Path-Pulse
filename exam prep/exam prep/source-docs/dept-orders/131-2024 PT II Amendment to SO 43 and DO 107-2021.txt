PARTH
AMENDMENT TO STANDING ORDER 43 AND
DEPARTMENTAL ORDER 107 of 2021
1. INFORMATION:
In keeping with the roles and responsibilities of the Ministry of Finance — Pension
Management Branch (PMB), and instructions from the Auditor General for the submission
of Pension and Leave Records, it is now imperative for the organization to submit Pension
Packages in the updated designated time frame.
2. INTENTION:
This order is intended to amend Standing Order No. 43 and Departmental Order 107 of 2021,
dated Monday 13th September, 2021 by updating the designated time frame for submission
of records.
3. AMENDMENT
1. Standing Order 43 Paragraph 37. (1) is hereby amended to read as follows:
37. (1) A Police Officer whose retirement is imminent or wishes to retire voluntarily,
shall apply in writing to the Commissioner of Police at least fifteen (15) months before
the date of retirement, with a view to having his benefits computed.
2. Departmental Order No. 107 of 2021, dated Monday 13th September, 2021 has
been amended by replacing the following: -
37. (1) A Police Officer whose retirement is imminent or wishes to retire voluntarily,
shall apply in writing to the Commissioner of Police at least six (6) months before the
date of retirement, with a view to having his benefits computed.
