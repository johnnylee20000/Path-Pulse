NEW_MEASURES FOR ACCESS TO AIRPORT TERMINALS AND ASSOCIATED
SPACES
Please be advised that with immediate effect and until further notice, the Airports Authority of
Trinidad and Tobago (“the Authority”) would be enforcing a strict facemask requirement for
persons entering the terminals and associated public spaces of the Piarco and ANR Robinson
International Airports. This measure is in accordance with national public health guidelines and
best practice aimed at mitigating the spread of the COVID-19 virus.
Accordingly, the Authority is requesting the assistance of the Commissioner of Police, as a
principal stakeholder in airport and border security, to have this measure communicated to your
personnel stationed at both airports. The authority also requests that your personnel, through your
office, be instructed to comply with these measures.
We thank you for your understanding and support during these challenging times and look forward
to continuing our partnerships as we move towards post COVID-19 recovery and return to normal
operations.
