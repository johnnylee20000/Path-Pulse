PART II
Opening of the Hunting Season and sale of State Game Licences
The Open Hunting Season 2019/2020 starts on 1‘ October, 2019 and the sale of State Game
Licences to the public commences on Monday 16" September, 2019 at the following Centers:
1. District Revenue Office, Siparia
2. Forestry Division, Rio Claro
3. District Revenue Office, Tunapuna
4. District Revenue Office, Chaguanas
5. Forestry Division, Long Circular Road, St James
6. Forestry Division, Balisier Avenue, Pleasantville, San Fernando
7. Forestry Division, Sangre Grande
8. District Revenue Office, Sangster Hill, Scarborough, Tobago
9. Inland Revenue Office, Roxborough, Tobago
It should be noted that all hunters must be in possession of the appropriate State Game Licences
to engage in this activity.
