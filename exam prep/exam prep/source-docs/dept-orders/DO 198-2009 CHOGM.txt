COMMONWEALTH HEADS OF GOVERNMENT MEETING 
November 27 to 30,2009 
1. INFORMATION 
The Republic of Trinidad and Tobago will host The Twenty-First Commonwealth Heads of 
Government Meeting (CHOGM) during the period 27- 30 November 2009. CHOGM, which is 
held every two years, will bring together fifty-one (51) Heads of States and Governments from 
Africa, the Americas, Asia, Europe, the Pacific and the Caribbean. 
The Commonwealth is a voluntary association of fifty-three (53) Countries that support each 
other and work together towards shared goals in democracy and development. 
The main objective of this biennial event is to discuss global issues and agree upon collective 
policies and initiatives. CHOGM is distinct from other international summits in that, Heads of 
States and Governments meet in an informal retreat setting where decisions are reached only by 
consensus. This year's theme "Partneriog for a more Equitable and Sustainable Future", 
is about bringing people closer together to share ideas and create partnerships to address some of 
the most critical issues of the 21st century. 
Heads of States and Governments attending the Meeting will be accompanied by their respective 
delegations, press and media personnel. Also attending will be representatives from international 
organizations and other specially invited dignitaries. 
As is customary, Her Majesty, Queen Elizabeth II, as Head of the Commonwealth, will pay a 
State Visit to the Republic of Trinidad and Tobago to address the opening ceremony. 
Accompanying her will be the Duke of Edinburgh. 
The Trinidad and Tobago Police Service, with assistance from other agencies of the Ministry of 
National Security is tasked with the role and responsibility for all security arrangements for 
CHOGM.

2. MISSION 
To ensure a safe and secure environment for the successful hosting ofCHOGM during the period 
November 20th- 30th, 2009. 
3. INTENT 
This Order is intended to inform police officers of the onerous responsibility of ensuring that 
CHOGM is conducted in an environment that is safe, secure and free from threats of sabotage, 
violence, disruption and terrorism while maintaining law and order. 
4. CHOGM ASSOCIATED EVENTS 
As ts customary, CHOGM associated events will be held prior to the commencement of 
CHOGM2009. 
a. The Commonwealth Youth Forum (CYF) 
Participation in the CYF is opened to youths between the ages of 15 and 25 years. It will be held 
during the period November 21st to 27th, 2009. The Forum programme will commence in 
Tobago and conclude in Trinidad. 
b. Tire Commonwealth Business Forum (CBF) 
The CBF, a major international business event which brings together some one thousand (1,000) 
businesses and government leaders from across the Commonwealth, will be held from November 
23rd to 26th, 2009 on the Cruise Liner, "Serenade of the Seas'', which will be docked in the Port 
of Spain Harbour. 
c. The C'ommonweahh People's Forum (CPF) 
The CPF, the largest civil society event in the Commonwealth, will be held from November 22"d 
to 26th, 2009. The opening ceremony will be held at Queen's Hall, while actual forum will be 
held at the Cascadia Hotel and Conference Centre in St. Ann's, Port of Spain. 
d. The Pre-CHOGM Meeting of Commonwealth Foreign Ministries 
The Pre-CHOGM Meeting of Commonwealth Foreign Ministries will be held at the Trinidad 
Hilton and Conference Centre in Port of Spain, Republic of Trinidad and Tobago on Wednesday 
25th and Thursday 26th November 2009. The Minister of Foreign Affairs of the Republic of 
Trinidad and Tobago accompanied by the Commonwealth Secretary General will address a news 
conference on Thursday 26 November 2009 on the deliberations of Foreign Ministries. 
5. THREAT ASSESSMENT 
The policing operation for CHOGM is focused on the NATIONAL and INTERNATIONAL 
THREAT LEVEL towards potential targets in order to reduce vulnerability. Close liaison will be 
maintained with other agencies in order that assessments or relevant intelligence are forwarded 
immediately to the Gold or Silver Commander for action .

6. METHOD 
The Gold, Silver and Bronze command structure will be used to ensure clarity and chain of 
command at the Strategic, Tactical and Operational levels. 
Assistant Commissioner of Polic~ Stephen WilJiams is the Gold Commander with his 
alternate Senior Superintendent, Lindon McMillan . 
The Silver Commanders will be responsible for tactical command and directing the 
implementation of the policing arrangements whilst ensuring that the objectives and plans in 
accordance with the operational order are fulfilled. 
Police Officers will be placed at:­
• All CHOGM Locations. 
• At every intersection along the Churchill Roosevelt Highway to control the entrances 
leading to the Highway 
• On all walkways and flyovers on the route during the motorcade to and from Port-of 
Spain. 
Police officers will be required to:-
• Search, declare safe and secure the principal venues and other specified locations. 
• Establish both fixed and flexible search procedures to prevent persons or articles posing a 
threat to safety and security from passing through controlled perimeter cordons. 
Police officers who have not been assigned specific summit duties shall continue to police 
their respective Divisions, Branches, Unit and Sections on heightened alert to prevent 
infractions of the law. 
7. ADMINISTRATION 
Effective 22"d to 30th November 2009 all police officers shall be detailed for duty on a twenty 
four (24) hour basis . 
All officers temporary assigned to Sectors for CHOGM duties shall return to their respective 
Divisions/Sections/Branches by 8:00am on 30th November, 2009 without fail. 
Leave restrictions shall continue until 8.00am on 1st December, 2009 •

APPENDIX 'A' 
SECURITY SECTORS: 
Sector 1 
This sector is responsible for the security of Heads of States and Governments; and other 
Delegates at Piarco International Airport. They must also provide security for the Aircrafts, the 
inner and outer perimeter ofthe Airport's compound including the routes around both the North 
and South terminals. 
Sector 2 
This sector is responsible for securing the primary routes along the Churchill Roosevelt 
Highway, Priority Bus Route, Eastern Main Road and the Beetham Highway. Therefore, mobile, 
static and foot patrols will be deployed along the main routes to deal with traffic situations that 
may arise during the passage of the various convoys. 
Sector 3 
This sector is responsible for securing all Hotels, National Diplomatic Centre, Medical Centres, 
National Academy for Performing Arts, (NAPA), the Port Facility (POS) and venues of other 
CHOGM related meetings and events, thereby providing perimeter security to vulnerable points, 
major utilities and installations at the venues. Additionally, mobile, foot and static patrols will 
be deployed to control all entrance and egress points at the respective venues. This sector will 
also maintain a composite Quick Response Team augmented by the availability of the TTPS 
Mounted Branch. 
Sector 5 
This sector is responsible for securing the South Western Area of Tobago in order to provide a 
safe environment for the successful hosting of the Commonwealth Youth Forum 7 and the visit 
by the Duke of Edinburgh. 
Island Site 
This component is responsible for securing the area of the Hyatt Regency Hotel and the 
International Financial Centre Towers and will provide a perimeter cordon around the Hyatt 
Regency Hotel and Conference Centre, referred to as the "Island Site. The Island Site will be 
totally closed to the public with access strictly limited to pass holders with the correct type of 
pass. 
Senior Investigating Officers (S.I.O) 
This unit is a new concept for our national landscape and is dedicated solely to the investigation 
of CHOGM related crimes. Established to deter, disrupt, arrest and prosecute any criminal 
perpetrator who is intent on committing any criminal act/s against participants ofthe meeting.
