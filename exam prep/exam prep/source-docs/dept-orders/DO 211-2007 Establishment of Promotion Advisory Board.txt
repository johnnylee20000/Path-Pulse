ESTABLISHMENT OF THE PROMOTION ADVISORY BOARD 
Departmental Order No. 205 dated 17th September 2007 is rescinded 
1. INFORMATION 
1: 1 In accordance with the requirements of the Police Service Act 2006, as amended by Act No 13 of 
2007 , the management of the Trinidad and Tobago Police Service has established the Promotion 
Advisory Board (P.A.B.) 
1:2 The Board shall conduct interviews and make recommendations to the Commissioner of Police 
in relation to promotions to the rank of Corporal, Sergeant and Inspector. 
1:3 The Promotion Advisory Board (PAB) shall comprise five (5) members namely:-
a) a Deputy Commissioner of Police , who shall be the chairman; 
b) an Assistant Commissioner of Police ; 
c) the Director Human Resources of the Police Service; 
d) a senior officer, who is not a police officer, from the Ministry of National Security, 
selected by the Minister; and 
e) an independent management consu ltant, appointed by the Commissioner. 
1 :4 The Commissioner shall engage a person who is not a police officer as Secretary to the Board. 
1:5 The appropriate recognized association may nominate a representative to be present as an 
observer at sittings of the Board . 
1:6 The Secretary to the Board shall keep a record of the ratings given to every police officer who 
appears before the Board. 
1:7 The Board shall sit as often as it thinks necessary, but at least once every three months to 
consider promotions and notice of each sitting shall be given in a timely manner to the 
representative referred to in 1.5.

3. METHODOLOGY - Promotion in the Second Division 
3: 1 The Promotion Advisory Board shall interview :-
a) an officer who has passed the qualifying examination for promotion and is recommended for 
promotion by the officer in charge of his Division or Branch; 
b) an officer who was allocated fifty (50) or more points at the previous interview; and 
c) an officer who is eligible under para.3 :3 
3:2 An officer shall not be interviewed by the Board unless he has been allocated forty or more 
points by the Board based on the criteria, other than the interview, listed in 3:5 
3:3 Subject to 3:2, an officer who is allocated less than sixty points is eligible to be interviewed at 
the next sitting of the Board. 
3:4 Every officer considered for promotion shalt be rated according to the criteria specified in 3:5 
and each officer who is allocated sixty or more points shalt be placed on an Order ofMerit List.

4. ADMINISTRATION 
4:1 Heads of Divisions/Branches shall ensure that all Performance Appraisal Reports are properly 
prepared and submitted in a timely manner. 
4:2 Heads of Divisions/Branches shall be required to make recommendations for promotion of 
officers under their command on the Candidate Assessment Form 
The recommendations shall be accompanied by a copy of the officers Performance Appraisal 
Report, certified by the Head ofDivision!Branch. 
5. COMMlJNICATION 
5: 1 Heads of Divisions/Branches shall be required to attend an orientation session in order to 
familiarize themselves with the new promotion system for promotion within the Second 
Division. 
5:2 Heads of Divisions/Branches shall in tum hold orientation sessions in the Division/Branch to 
educate officers of the new promotion system for promotion within the Second Division. 
5:3 Heads of Divisions/Branches shall adhere strictly with requirements of this Order. 
6. ATTIRE AND APPEARANCE 
6:1 Police Officers are reminded of the need to dress appropriately to attend interviews of the Board . 
6:2 All Police Officers (unless authorized to wear plainclothes) shall appear before the Board attired 
in Patrol Order number I . 
6:3 Police Officers who are authorized to wear plain clothes shall appear before the Board properly 
attired. Male officers shall wear sober-coloured lounge suit and tie whilst female officers shall 
wear business attire. 
7. CONCLUSION 
7:1 The system outlined in this order is designed to identify, select and promote the most efficient 
and effective officers to leadership posjtions thus ensuring that the foundation is laid for effective 
succession planning. 
7:2 Succession planning is the benchmark of effective management. The development of our human 
resource is necessary for the continued growth and improvement of the organisation. The 
conceptualisation of a more efficient, productive and effective Police Service has marufested 
itself in the transformation process currently being undertaken throughout the service. A 
promotion system based on merit is an essential part of this process.
