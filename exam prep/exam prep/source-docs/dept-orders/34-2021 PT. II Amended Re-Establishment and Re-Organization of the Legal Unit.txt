PART II
AMENDED RE-ESTABLISHMENT and RE- ORGANIZATION OF THE LEGAL UNIT

The Re- Establishment and Re-Organization of the Legal Unit of the Trinidad and Tobago Police

Service (Departmental Order No. 11 of 2021, dated Thursday 21% January, 2021) has been amended to

include the following, succeeding paragraph 6.3.

6.3.1 Legal Officers and Police Officers shall submit all requested documentation, files, reports and
correspondence directly to the Police Complaints Authority in a timely manner pursuant to
Section (22) (2) of the Police Complaints Authority Act Chapter 15:05.

Failure to do so shall be considered disobedience to orders.
