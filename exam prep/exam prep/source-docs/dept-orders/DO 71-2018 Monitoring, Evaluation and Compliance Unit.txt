PART II
ESTABLISHMENT OF THE MONITORING, EVALUATION AND COMPLIANCE
UNIT IN THE TRINIDAD AND TOBAGO POLICE SERVICE
1. Establishment
The Commissioner of Police has taken a strategic decision to establish a Monitoring, Evaluation
and Compliance Unit within the Trinidad and Tobago Police Service (TTPS) with effect from
May 1%‘, 2017. The Unit falls under the ambit of Executive Management Services (EMS) in the
Office of the Commissioner of Police.
2. Mandate
The Monitoring Evaluation and Compliance Unit has as its mandate to establish and strengthen
results, based on monitoring and evaluation systems and frameworks within the TTPS to support
the use of evidence based information for decision-making across the organization.
3. Role and Functions
i. To facilitate the establishment of organizational performance measures and targets in
collaboration with the TTPS Executive and Heads of Units, Sections and Branches.
ii. To collect and analyze information on results on an ongoing basis.
iii. To collaborate with relevant stakeholders (Internal and External) to enable collation and
compilation of data and statistics for the preparation of reports.
iv. Provide input into the design and development of proposals for assessments, evaluations and
special studies relating to TTPS performance.
v. Provide input into the design, development or review of policies to enhance achievement of key
results.
vi. To supervise or assist with the supervision of internal evaluation activities or external evaluation
consultants contracted by the TTPS as necessary.
vii. To conduct evaluation assessments of specific TTPS initiatives and lead internal cross
departmental teams in such exercises as necessary.

viii. To assist Heads of Units, Sections and Branches to develop or enhance data collection methods
and tools for adequate monitoring of operations, initiatives and programmes.
ix. To participate in the regular review of progress towards achieving outcomes by the Executive and
Senior Management.
x. To develop and support tracking mechanisms which strengthen compliance with Departmental
Orders, Standing Orders and Policies of the TTPS.
xi. To build capacity in monitoring and evaluation across the TTPS, through training and coaching
of staff across Divisions, Stations, Sections, Branches and Unit level.
4. Access to Data
The Monitoring, Evaluation and Compliance Unit shall be afforded access to the data necessary
to perform the functions as outlined above; including data housed in the Versadex system and
other relevant records kept at the Divisions, Station, Section, Branches and Unit level.
5. Sanctioned Strength
The Sanctioned strength of the Monitoring, Evaluation and Compliance Unit shall be as follows:
Manager, Monitoring, Evaluation and Compliance Unit
Monitoring, Evaluation and Compliances Officers
Business Operations Assistant I
6. Location and Contact Information
The Monitoring, Evaluation and Compliance Unit is located on the 5‘ Floor, Police
Administration Building, corner Edward and Sackville Streets, Port of Spain. The Unit can be
contacted by telephone at 612-2467 or 612-2719 and email at mecu@ttps.gov.tt.
