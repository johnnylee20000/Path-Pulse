PART II
NEW ORGANIZATIONAL CHART AND EXECUTIVE OFFICE TITLE CHANGE
Organizational Chart of the Trinidad and Tobago Police Service
The existing reporting structure of the Trinidad and Tobago Police Service has been reviewed and
a new Organization Chart has been developed to reflect the new lines of reporting responsibility.
Attached at Appendix A is a copy of the new Organization Chart. Title changes have also been
made to some positions.
Deputy Commissioners of Police
The titles of the Offices of the Deputy Commissioners of Police have been changed as follows:
1. From Deputy Commissioner of Police ‘Crime and Support’ te Deputy Commissioner of
Police ‘Intelligence and Investigations’.
2. From Deputy Commissioner of Police Administration to Deputy Commissioner of Police
‘Administration and Operational Support’.
The title of Deputy Commissioner of Police ‘Operations’ remains the same.

Re-Designation of Assistant Commissioner of Police Central
The title of Assistant Commissioner of Police ‘Central’ as designated in Departmental Order #6
of 2017 has been Re-designated Assistant Commissioner of Police ‘Administration’ with effect
from 2" February, 2019.
The Assistant Commissioner of Police ‘Administration’ has Executive responsibility for:-

1. Human Resource Branch

2. Finance Branch

3. Promotion Advisory Board

4. Police Hospital
Re-Designation of Assistant Commissioner of Police Central
The Assistant Commissioner of Police ‘South’ as designated in Departmental Order #6 of 2017
has been re-designated Assistant Commissioner of Police ‘South Central’ and has Executive
responsibility for:-

1. Southern Division

2. Central Division

3. South Western Division
Civilian Heads and Provost
Included in the new Organization Chart are the civilian Heads and the Provost. The persons in
these positions all provide administrative and strategic support to and on behalf of the
Commissioner of Police.
Authority
Section 193 (1) (b) of the Police Service Regulations 2007
The publication of this Departmental Order amends Part II of Departmental Order 6 of 2017,
published on Thursday 12" January, 2017.
