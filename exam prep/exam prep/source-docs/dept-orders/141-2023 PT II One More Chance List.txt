No. 141 Departmental Order PART I & Il - NIL
PART-II
Results of the 2022 Promotional Examinations to the ranks of Corporal and Sergeant
The Police Service Examination Board advises that candidates who sat the 2022 Promotional
Examinations to the rank of Corporal and Sergeant, and were placed on the One More Chance
List, vide Departmental Orders No. 68 of 2023 and No. 69 of 2023 respectively, their marks will
be held for the 2024 Promotional Examinations and the candidates will only be required to sit
the failed component of the respective examination.
Commissioner of Police
