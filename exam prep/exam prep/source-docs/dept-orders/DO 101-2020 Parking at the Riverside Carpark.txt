Parking at the Riverside Carpark
In an effort to reduce rental expenditure and with the demonstrated underuse of
the Riverside Plaza carpark, the decision was taken in 2019 to reduce the
number of parking spaces the TTPS would use. We communicated same to the
landlord who has now written to TTPS indicating that parking on floors 1-5
must desist immediately.
As such, parking for TTPS at the Riverside Carpark is restricted to floors 6 and
7 ONLY with immediate effect.
