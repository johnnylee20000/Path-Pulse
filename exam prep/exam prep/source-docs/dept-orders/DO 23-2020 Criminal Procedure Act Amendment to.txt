3. Rule 5 amended
3. Rule 5.4 is amended by revoking paragraph (2) and substituting the
following paragraph:
“(2) The Court may
(a) issue a warrant without giving the parties an opportunity
to make representations; and
(b) withdraw a warrant at any time before the determination of
the matter in which it was tssued.”

4. New Rule 22A inserted
+. The Rules are amended by inserting after Rule 22 the following
new Rule:
“22A Documents
22A (1) So far as is practicable, every document prepared
for use in the Supreme Court and Magistrates Court must be on
“letter size paper: approximately 11 inches long by 8.5 inches
wide. Margins of 1" (25mm) must be left at the top and bottom
and of 1.5" (38mm) at each side.

(2) Every originating application to be filed at the Criminal
Registry of the Supreme Court or at the Magistrates Court
must be accompanied by acompleted Form 5 of the
Schedule for a New Application.

(3) Every existing application to be filed at
the Criminal Registry of the Supreme
Court or at the Magistrates Court must be
accompanied by acompleted Form 6 of the
Schedule for an Existing Application.”.
