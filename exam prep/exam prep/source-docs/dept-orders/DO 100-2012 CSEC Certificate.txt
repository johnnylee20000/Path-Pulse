PART II 
On recommendations of the Police Examination Board, members of the Service writing the 2012 Police 
Promotion Examination are reminded of the standards for exemption from writing the English Language 
component in the Promotion Examination for Constable to Corporal and Corporal to Sergeant: 
(a) Candidates who attained a CSEC Certificate Grade ill in English Language prior to June 
1998 or GCE Ordinary Level Grades D-E were not eligible for exemption from writing 
the English Language component for further qualifying examination in the Police 
Service. 
(b) Candidate who were previously exempted by the Commissioner ofPolice having attained 
the qualifications mentioned at (a) above, would be required to write the English 
Language component in the Police Promotion Examination for promotion in the Police 
Service (Second Division). 
Additionally, these conditions at (a) and (b) would be in effect for aU future Examinations for promotion 
in the Second Division.
