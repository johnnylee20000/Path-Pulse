Proclamation of the Administration of Justice Electronic (Electronic Monitoring) Act
No 11 of 2012 and the Administration of Justice (Electronic Monitoring) Amendment
Act No 11 of 2020
This Departmental Order serves to inform members of the Trinidad and Tobago Police
service of the following: -
The Administration of Justice Electronic (Electronic Monitoring) Act No 11 of 2012 and the
Administration of Justice (Electronic Monitoring) (Amendment) Act No 11 of 2020, were
proclaimed by Her Excellency Paula-Mae Weekes, O.R.T.T, President of the Republic of
Trinidad and Tobago on September 18", 2020.
Copies of Legal Notices No 323 and No 324 are attached.
Commissioner of Police

LEGAL Notice No. 323
REPUBLIC OF TRINIDAD AND TOBAGO
No. 27 of 2020.
By Her Excellency PAULA-MAE WEEKES, O.R.T.T.,
President of the Republic of Trinidad and Tobago
and Commander-in-Chief of the Armed Forces.
[L.S.]
PAULA-MAE WEEKES
President
A PROCLAMATION
WHEREAS it is provided by section 1(2) of the Administration of Justice
(Electronic Monitoring) Act, 2012 (Act No. 11 of 2012) (hereinafter
referred to as “the Act”) that the Act shall come into operation on such
date as is fixed by the President by Proclamation:

And whereas it is expedient that all sections of the Act come into
operation:

Now, therefore, I, PAULA-MAE WEEKES, President as aforesaid, do
hereby fix the 18th day of September, 2020, as the date on which the
Act shall come into operation.

Given under my Hand and the Seal of
the President of the Republic of
Trinidad and Tobago, at the Office of
the President, St. Ann’s, this 18th day of
September, 2020.
PRINTED AND PUBLISHED BY THE GOVERNMENT PRINTER, CARONI
REPUBLIC OF TRINIDAD AND TOBAGO—2020
