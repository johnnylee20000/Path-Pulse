PART II
Designation of the Youth Training Centre at Golden Grove as a Child Rehabilitation Centre
With immediate effect the Youth Training Centre at Golden Grove has been designated a Child Rehabilitation
Centre by Legal Notice No. 39, in accordance with the Child Rehabilitation Centre Act, Chapter 13:05.
The Interim Child Rehabilitation Centre established at St Michael’s Industrial School for boys has not been
designated under the Act and no longer has the authority to house or accept boys on remand.
Hereunder is a reproduction of the Legal Notice No. 39.
