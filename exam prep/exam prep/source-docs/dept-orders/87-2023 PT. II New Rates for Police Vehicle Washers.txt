PART Il
RATE INCREASE FOR THE WASHING AND CLEANING OF POLICE VEHICLES
INTENTION
To work in conjunction with Departmental Order 25/2009 and not rescind it. Also to indicate
the new approved rates effective 1 October, 2022 for persons employed in the capacity of
washing and cleaning of police vehicles, assigned to the Trinidad and Tobago Police Service.
JUSTIFICATION
The last effective rate increase for washers of police vehicles, assigned to the Trinidad and
Tobago Police Service occurred fourteen (14) years ago in 2008. It must be noted that this new
increase in rate, is made due to the upturn in the cost of living, which has become more evident
in recent times. Washers have been pushed further into a disadvantageous position, by the fact
that the cost of material and consumables which they are required to purchase, have had price
increases several times over the past years. It must be borne in mind that these new rates fall
below the prevailing market rates; this is because the Trinidad and Tobago Police Service is a
captive market for the washers with guaranteed work. Also these washers are not provided with
cleaning materials and are therefore required to provide same on a daily basis.
STRATEGIES
1. Washers are required to wash the outside of vehicles (not the inside) although at times
police may make requests for the inside to be cleaned as well, but under supervision.
2. Each washer would be granted a monthly sum of One Hundred Dollars TT ($100.00)
to aid in the purchasing of materials for the washing and polishing of vehicles. This
would aid in the offsetting of the cost of cleaning materials.
3. Additionally police vehicles are to be polished once every six (6) months or on the
occasion of a national event.
