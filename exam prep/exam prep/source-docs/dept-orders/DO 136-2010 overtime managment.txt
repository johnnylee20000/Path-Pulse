Departmental Order 273, dated 23/11/2001, paragraphs 4:6 and 4:7 
is amended to read:-
4:6 The Divisional Head, once satisfied that the claim is genuine, shall 
sign and refer it to the Senior Superintendent Finance Branch 
who shall be responsible for authorizing the payment of overtime 
claims.

to the Senior Superintendent Finance Branch. must include the 
claimant's number, rank and name, as well as the periods of 
overtime in relation to the claims.
