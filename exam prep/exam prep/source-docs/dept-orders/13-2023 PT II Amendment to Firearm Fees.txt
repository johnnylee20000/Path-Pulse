No. 13 Departmental Order PART I
Amendment to Firearms Fees and Authorization for Revenue Collection by the Comptroller of Accounts
1. INFORMATION
Lat In accordance with the Firearms Act Ch. 16:01, as amended by Act No. 21 of 2022, with effect from January
01, 2023, all firearms fees have been increased by 100%, (see appendix 1). Notwithstanding, arrears of
revenue payable on all firearms for all previous years inclusive of 2022, remains in effect.
2 Receipt Books
2.1 The existing receipt books for the collection of revenue for all firearms in which the old fees are printed
will remain in use, pending the publication by the Government Printers of the new receipt books reflecting
the increases. , . oi
In the interim, the Treasury Director has approved the following guidelines which must be adhered to when
receiving all firearm related revenue, using the existing Receipt Books. These guidelines will remain in
effect until the new receipt books with the amended fees are made available.
a Issuance of Receipts
When collecting the amended fees, two receipts will be issued for each transaction to reflect the 100%
increase, with respect to the new fees only. For example, when issuing receipts for the renewal of Firearm
User’s License, two (2) receipts are to be written up, with each reflecting the sum of $150.00 each.
Bad When preparing receipts, ensure that the necessary information on both receipts are filled and the following
are stated on the respective receipts, (See appendix 2):
“For the year 2023”, must be written on the top of each receipt.
“Part Payment” must be written near the total printed on the receipt
“1 of 2” must be written on the 1* receipt
“2 of 2” must be written on the 2" receipt
The 2" receipt number must be written on the 1‘ receipt
The 1* receipt number must be written on the 2™ receipt
RP All previous year fees will remain in effect and one Receipt per pervious years still applies which can be
collected on the same Receipt Book.
