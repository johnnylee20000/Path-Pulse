1. In this Standing Order:  
“Sanctioned Strength” means the established strength approved by 
Cabinet for the Police Service. 
 
“Posting” means the initial movement of an officer from the Police 
Academy to a Division /Branch / Section/ Unit or, the movement of an 
officer within a Division/ Branch/ Section/ Unit. 
 
“Transfer” means the movement of an officer from a Division/ Branch/ 
Section/ Unit to another D ivision/ Branch/ Section/ Unit. Transfers can 
either be temporary or permanent. 
 
 
2. The Commiss ioner of Police shall be responsible for the   
distribution of the  Sanctioned  Strength  assigned to the Trinidad and  
Toba go Police Service. 
 
3. The Commiss ioner of Police shall publish the  most current  
Sanctioned  Strength of the  Trinidad and Tobago Police Service  in               
Departmental  Orders  on or before June 30th annually . 
 
4. (1) The Commiss ioner of Police shall be resp onsible  for  the 
postings an d tra nsfers of all  police  officers to Division/ Branches/ 
Sections/ Units an d  these  shall  be published in Depart ment al Orders 
within one month of such posting and /or transfer . 
 
 
         (2)  Postings  within Divisions / Branches/ Sections /Units 
shall  be made by the Officer - in- Charge of th e Division /Branch / 
Section / Unit .  
          (3) U nless specifically authorized  by the Commissioner of 
Police,  no  officer shall be posted/trans ferred to any Specialist 
Branch /Section / Unit  until he has completed  at  least   three   (3)  
years  service .
