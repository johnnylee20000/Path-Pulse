1. Fire fighting operations are the responsibility of the Trinidad
and Tobago Fire Service.
2. Police Officers at the scene of a fire shall be responsible for
the following:
(a) preserving order;
(b) regulating traffic;
(c) investigating the report of the fire.
3. On receipt of the report of a fire, whether extinguished or
not, it shall be the responsibility of the officer receiving the report
to do the following:
(a) inform the nearest Fire Station;
(b) inform his senior officer at Station of the report.
4. The senior Second Division Officer present at Station, on
receipt of such report shall—
(a) despatch personnel to the scene;
(b) inform the Officer-in-charge of the Division and the
First Division Officer in charge of the area.
5. The senior Police Officer detailed for duty at the scene of  a
fire shall—
(a) inform his senior officer of the situation existing at
the scene;
(b) detail other officers to—
(i) divert traffic away from the scene of the
fire;
(ii) prevent congestion of the streets to and
from the scene of the fire to allow free
movement of fire units, ambulances and
other vehicles connected with the fire fight-
ing operations;
(iii) keep bystanders a safe distance away from
the fire to allow firemen to operate and to
avoid damages to fire equipment;

(iv) prevent unauthorised persons from enter-
ing the building on fire. Authorised persons
shall enter the building only with the per-
mission of the senior Fire Officer present;
(v) prevent looting of property;
(vi) preserve order generally. “Crush Barriers”
are to be used where necessary;
(c) provide the following officers with information relat-
ing to the fire on his return to Station:
(i) Assistant Commissioner of Police in charge
of the area;
(ii) Assistant Commissioner of Police “Crime”;
(iii) Officer in charge of the Division.
6. It shall be the duty of a Police Officer investigating a report
of fire to—
(a) consult the senior Fire Officer at the scene of the
fire;
(b) liaise with the Fire Prevention Officer within  twenty-
four (24) hours of the receipt of the report to ascer-
tain the cause or origin of the fire and to determine
whether there is need to request the services of a
Scientific Officer to visit the scene;
(c) arrange for a Fire Prevention Officer to visit the
scene with the Scientific Officer and himself;
(d) ensure that the scene of the fire is protected against
interference, pending the completion of enquires by
the Scientific Officers;
(e) secure any evidence found on the scene of the fire;
(f) request the services of a Police photographer;
(g) interview persons and collate the following       infor-
mation:
(i) incidents immediately before the outbreak
of the fire;

(l) mark off the occurrence when the file is returned

instructions to close: “No Further Police Action”.
However, if the instructions are to prosecute, the
Investigator will cancel the occurrence and enter the
crime disclosed in the appropriate register.
7. On perusal of the file, if the Officer in charge of the Division
is satisfied that the matter should be dealt with by the Coroner as
stipulated in section 18 of the Coroner’s Act, Chap. 6:04, he shall
submit the file to the Director of Public Prosecutions through the
Assistant Commissioner “Crime”.
