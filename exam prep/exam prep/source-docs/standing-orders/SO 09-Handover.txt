1. In this Standing Order:  
“Daily Handover” refers to the transfer of responsibility between Second 
Division Officers relieving each other on a daily basis.  
 
“General Handover” refers to the transfer of command between: 
(a) Executive Officers; 
(b) First Division Officers; or 
(c) Second Division Officers responsible for Stations and Sections. 
 
“Handover ” refers  to an  entry made  in  a register  wherein  Government 
Property and other Property are  passed from  one police  officer  to  another,  
including  a  written  report  on the  state  of command. 
 
“Property” refers to any animal, article or thing which has come into the possession of 
the police, either through finding, seizure or any other lawful means. 
 
“Register” refers to a written or electronic record of 
events, transactions and information.
