1. “Licensed Premises” refer to any building or part thereof for
which a licence/permit is issued by the relevant authority to carry
out any trade/business as required by the terms of such licence.

3. A register known as the Licensed Premises Register shall be
kept at each Station. See Appendix “A” attached.
4. This register shall be kept and maintained in the following
manner:
(a) The pages shall be numbered consecutively in ink
and a certificate entered on the last page of the
register.
(b) At the top of the pages, enter the current year and
the type of licensed premises.
(c) Enter each type of licensed premises under a
separate page and number them chronologically.
(d) Upon the expiry date of each type of licence or on an
annual basis, whichever is applicable to each type of
licence, the entries are to be carried forward to the
new year under their separate heading.
5. Licensed premises shall be visited at least once a month by
an officer not under the rank of Corporal, but with respect to
Registered Clubs, visits shall be done by all Inspectors, Second
Division Officer-in-charge of Station or Police Officers appointed
by a First Division Officer.

IQUOR LICENCES ACT, CHAP. 84:10
7. (1) The Officer in charge of a Division on receipt of an
application for a new or renewal of a licence from the District
Court, shall send a copy of such application attached to the
prescribed form set out at Appendix “B” to the Second Division
Officer-in-charge of the Station in which the premises is located
for enquiries.
(2) The Second Division Officer-in-charge of the Station on
receipt of the documents mentioned at paragraph (1) above,
should make enquiries or appoint an officer not below the rank of
Corporal to make enquiries into such application and submit his
recommendations to him.
(3) The Second Division Officer of the Station upon comple-
tion of his investigation, shall submit his findings and recommen-
dations to the Officer in charge of the Division on the prescribed
form.
(4) The Officer in charge of the Division on receipt of the
findings and recommendations from the Second Division Officer-
in-charge of the Station shall do one of the following:
(a) Where the enquiries are completed to his satisfac-
tion, submit a copy of his recommendations to the
Clerk of the Peace in the Court where the matter is
to be heard.
(b) Where the enquiries are not completed to his satis-
faction, return same to the Second Division Officer-
in-charge of the Station for further enquiries and at
the same time inform the Officer in charge of Court
and Process Branch, requesting a new date for
hearing. In the case of applications outside of the
County of St. George West, inform the Court
Prosecutor to request same.

(c) Where there is an objection, ensure that the police

(d) Where the application is for a new Liquor Licence,

8. Where the hearing of the application is listed within the
County of St. George West, a copy of the application is to be sent
to the Officer in charge of the Court and Process Branch with a
copy of recommendations submitted to the Clerk of the Peace.
In all other instances a copy of the recommendations shall be
sent to the Court Prosecutor of the Court, where the application is
to be heard.
9. (1) Objection to the grant of licences may be made by
members of the public or police officers.
(2) The officer investigating the application for renewal of a
licence or the grant of a new licence, if he wishes to object shall do
so in accordance with the grounds set out at sections 21 and 30 of
the Liquor Licences Act, Chap. 84:10 and sections 21
A and 21C.
(3) Where any member of the public wishes to object and it
is brought to the knowledge of the Investigating Officer or
another officer attached to the Station which controls the district
in which the licensed premises or proposed licensed premises is
located, he shall record a statement from such objector or
objectors.
(4) The statement from an objector must include the
grounds for objection by such objector.
(5) The objector’s statement with a covering report and
recommendations shall be submitted immediately to the Officer in
charge of the Division.
(6) The officer shall deal with the matter in any of the
manner set out at paragraph 7(4) of this Order.
