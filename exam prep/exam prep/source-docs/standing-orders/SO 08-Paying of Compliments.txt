3. (1) A police officer  dressed in uniform shall salute in the 
following manner in one fluent move ment:  
 raise the right arm sideways until horizontal, 
straighten fingers and thumb, keeping them together, 
with palm facing to the front;  
 bend the right elbow keeping  the hand and wrist 
straight, until the tip of forefinger is one inch above 
the right eye, the palm facing to the front;  
 immediately force the elbow forward and curl the 
fingers and thumb into a fist;  
 force the right hand down to the side of the body.  
 
 (2)A police officer who has injured his right hand or 
arm and is unable to salute in accordance with  section 1 (1) 
shall salute with his left hand.
