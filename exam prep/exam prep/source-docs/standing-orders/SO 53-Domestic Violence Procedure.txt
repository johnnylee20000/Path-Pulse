1. In this Order—
(a) “Domestic Violence” refers to the physical, sexual,
emotional, psychological or financial abuse commit-
ted by a person against a spouse, child and any other
person who is a member of the household or a depen-
dant;
(b) “Domestic Violence Offence” refers to one of
the offences listed in the First Schedule of Act No. 27
of 1999;
(c) “Protection Order” refers to an Order made by a
Court, containing a set of prohibitions or directions
outlining on what a Respondent/Defendant should or
should not engage (Section 5);
(d) “Interim Protection Order” is made to afford protec-
tion to an applicant pending the hearing and
determination of the proceedings (Section 8);
(e) “Signed Undertaking” refers to a statement given
under oath by a respondent wherein it is indicated
that the respondent will not engage in conduct
specified in the application (Section 9);
(f) “Power of Arrest” refers to the power of the police to
restrain a person’s liberty without warrant where he
believes on reasonable grounds that a person has
committed a breach of a Protection Order (Section
24);
(g) “Bond of Good Behaviour” refers to an order by the
court requiring a defendant to be of good behaviour
for a period not exceeding six (6) months (Section
25);
(h) “Bail” refers to the release of a person on recogni-
zance in keeping with the Bail Act, No. 18 of 1994.
2. The Second Division Officer in charge of Modus Operandi
and Records Bureau shall maintain a National Domestic Violence
Register.

3. The Head of the Division shall open and maintain in each
station in his Division—
(a) Protection Order/Interim Protection Order/signed
undertaking Registers in which shall be drawn up
and used in accordance with Appendix “A”;
(b) Domestic Violence Register which is a duplicate
register in which shall be recorded a brief account of
the report including all pertinent information as
required by Form 7 of the Act.
4. The Second Division Officer in charge of a Station/Police
Post shall—
(a) ensure that the registers are checked and initialled
daily;
(b) certify that all reports of Domestic Violence have
been investigated;
(c) ensure that an investigator is detailed to investigate
each report;
(d) submit a monthly return of Domestic Violence
Offences on the prescribed forms to the Head of
Division within three (3) days after the previous
month (See Appendix B).
5. The Head of the Division shall submit to the Commissioner
of Police within forty-eight (48) hours the return at (d) along with
any other information necessary for the notice of the
Commissioner of Police.
6. The Police Officer to whom a report of Domestic Violence is
made shall—
(a) record the report in the station diary or in his
pocket diary, if he is not at station;
(b) inform the Senior Second Division Officer at station.
7. The Senior Second Division Officer to whom a report of
Domestic Violence is referred shall—
(a) appoint an investigator;

(b) render assistance where there is personal injury;
(c) contact a counsellor from the Community Policing
Section as soon as is practicable;
(d) ensure the report is entered in the register and cross
referenced with other entries in all appropriate
registers;
(e) ensure all interviews are conducted in private;
(f) ensure that the victim of domestic violence is
afforded comfort.
8. The Second Division Officer appointed to investigate a report
of Domestic Violence shall—
(a) complete the Domestic Violence Occurrence (DVO)
Form in duplicate ensuring that all information
necessary are recorded;
(b) submit the original Domestic Violence Occurrence
Form (Appendix C) to the Head of Division within
three (3) days of the report being recorded;
(c) write up all registers and books indicating the status
of the investigation;
(d) record statements and initiate criminal proceedings
where necessary.
9. The Head of the Division to whom a DVO Form and other
documents have been submitted shall ensure these documents are
transmitted to the Assistant Commissioner in charge of his
command.
10. Second Division Officers attached to the E999 Command
Centre on receipt of an emergency domestic violence call shall—
(a) despatch a vehicle to the location of the report;
(b) remain online with the victim/informant until the
response unit arrives at the location;
(c) provide assistance and comfort to the victim/
informant;
(d) solicit all information necessary to assist the victim/
informant.

11.The Senior Second Division Officer in the response unit on
arrival at the scene shall—
(a) inform the Command Centre of the unit’s arrival;
(b) assess the situation and report the status to the
Command Centre;
(c) record all relevant information and seek to contain
the scene;
(d) report all information in person to the Second
Division Officer in charge of the station in the
district where the victim/informant resides.
12. The Second Division Officer in charge of the station to whom
an E999 Officer reports shall—
(a) ensure that all relevant information are received in
the station diary;
(b) ensure the registers are written up in a chrono-
logical manner;
(c) appoint an investigator;
(d) do all that is required of him in paragraph 4.
13. (1) Police Officers are empowered by section 23 of the Act to
enter premises without a warrant.
(2) After the exercise of such powers of entry, the Police
Officer shall submit a written report to the Head of the Division
in charge of the area where the premises are situated. The report
shall contain—
(a) the reasons for entering the premises without
warning;
(b) the offence being committed or about to be
committed;
(c) the manner in which the investigation was
conducted; and
(d) the measures taken to ensure the protection and
safety of the person at risk.

(3) The Head of Division shall submit the report to the
Commissioner of Police within twenty-four (24) hours of receipt of
the report.
14. Police Officer who have entered premises without warrant
shall—
(a) give assistance to a person who has suffered injury;
(b) ensure the welfare and safety of a child who may be
on the premises;
(c) prevent any further breach of the law;
(d) arrest any person against whom it is alleged
committed a domestic violence offence.
15. General Progress Reports—
(a) Police Officers are advised that the Concluding
Progress Form shall be used for progress informa-
tion with respect to domestic violence, minor offences
and other matters in the following cases:
(i) a crime or incident is closed;
(ii) protection order have been issued;
(iii) where counselling is needed or is arranged;
(b) Police Officers are to ensure that the decisions at
(i), (ii) and (iii) above are communicated to the
informant/victim.
16. General—
(a) reports of domestic violence in which criminal
offences are identified shall be classified as S.R.C.,
M.C. or M.O. as the case may be;
(b) where reports do not reveal a crime they shall be
classified as other Domestic Violence Matters
(ODVM);
(c) the monthly serial number must precede the
relevant classification in column 2 of the Station
Diary and numbered consecutively;
