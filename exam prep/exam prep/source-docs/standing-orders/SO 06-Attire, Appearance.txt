7. All police officer s shall wear whistles when in uniform — 
(a) In the case of First Division Officers , Inspect  or s and 
Sergean ts, such whist les shall be att ached t o a dark 
blue lanyard. 
(b) In the case of Corporals and Constables, such whistles 
shall be att ached t o a nickelplated chain. 
 
8. (1) An officer below the rank of Inspector shall wear his Service 
Number and Name Tag affixed to the right breast of the uniform, with the 
name tag placed immediately above the top line of the pocket and the service 
number about one half inch above the name tag. 
 (2)An officer of or above the rank of Inspector, shall wear his Name 
Tag affixed to the right breast of the uniform immediately above the top line 
of the pocket. 
 (3)Service Numbers and Name Tags of white embroidered letters on a 
black background shall be sewn onto: 
 (a) tactical uniforms just above the front right breast pocket 
with the name tag placed immediately above the top line of the 
pocket and the Service Number about one inch above the Name Tag. 
 (b) bullet proof vests at an appropriate position which would 
not impair the visibility of the Service Number and Name Tag. 
 (c) police sweaters, jerseys and fluorescent/reflective vests 
in the region of the front right breast. 
 (4)Rain cloaks shall display Service Numbers and Name Tags in white 
permanent ink on the black side and black permanent ink on the fluorescent/ 
reflective side, in the region of the right breast pocket. 
 (5)The individual letters and numbers of all Service Numbers and 
Name Tag s shall be a size, dimension  and pattern which allow the easy 
recognition of an officer’s Service Number and Name from a reasonable 
distance. 
 
9. (1) For uniformed police officers, badges shall be worn on such part 
of their uniform and “Orders of Dress” as de signated by the Commissioner 
of Police. 
     (2) Police officers dressed in plainclothes (with the exception of those 
assigned to highly sensitive and covert units) shall wear their badge on 
such part of their civilian dress as designated by the Commissioner of 
Police.
